01

Segregation fails at the last metre

People want to sort. But "dry, wet, hazardous" is abstract at 7am with a yoghurt tub in hand. Confusion becomes contamination, and one wet bag ruins a whole sack of paper.

Fix: identify item/lot + direct segregation guidance
02

The informal sector is invisible

Kabadiwalas already run Delhi's recycling. But they work on phone calls and memory, with no price transparency, no route, and no proof of what they collected.

Fix: give collectors a network & verified digital passport
03

Bins are collected blind

Trucks visit every bin on a fixed loop, half of them empty. Fuel is burnt, time is lost, and overflowing bins still get missed because nobody knows the fill level.

Fix: fill sensors + route optimisation

Seven steps. Waste to verified impact.

A closed loop where material is matched to the nearest collector already segregated, handed upward to recyclers, and dual-verified at every exchange.

          Pilot reward

          Verified-handoff streak

          A streak counts verified handoffs, not app opens. This is a pilot reward, not guaranteed income.

          • 4 handoffs a month. Only one per day counts. The receiver must confirm the weight and material.
          • Month 1 pays ₹20, month 2 pays ₹60, month 3 pays ₹100. Each is that month’s payout — not a bonus stacked on the previous one. Total across three months: ₹180.
          • One grace week. A single missed week does not wipe progress; a longer gap resets the streak.
          • Anti-fraud. Same generator–collector pairs spiking, impossible weights, reused photos and GPS/time mismatches are flagged. Flagged rows do not count.
          • Payout. Starts as points or a voucher. Real-money UPI later goes through a licensed payment partner, not a wallet we run.
          • Capped pool. Each month has a fixed reward pool; if it runs out, payouts are proportional.
          Demo values. No money moves in this build — the card shows the rule, not a balance.
